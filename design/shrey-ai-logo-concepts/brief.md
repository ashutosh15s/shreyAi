# Shrey AI mascot logo concepts

## Working brief

- **Brand:** Shrey AI, a desktop virtual assistant for web search, live information, files, and everyday computer tasks.
- **Audience:** people who want a capable, approachable assistant on their computer.
- **Personality:** helpful, clever, calm, and dependable.
- **Primary use:** small desktop app icon and the Shrey AI wordmark in the interface.
- **Constraint:** create a new mascot identity, distinct from the reused Ace mark; the symbol must read in one colour and at small sizes.

## Concepts explored

Cursor mouse; signal wren; pocket bot; fox with a file-shaped ear; compass moth; raccoon with a speech-shaped mask; otter with a looping workflow tail; starling built from open chat brackets; desk gecko; and a small rover with a listening antenna.

## Concepts presented

1. **Milo, the cursor mouse** — a friendly mouse whose lower-right silhouette resolves into a pointer, connecting a familiar mascot with desktop interaction.
2. **Pip, the signal wren** — a compact bird whose forward beak acts like a pointer, suggesting search and discovery.
3. **Dot, the pocket bot** — a small assistant character with a listening antenna and a simple face, designed to feel like a companion rather than a tool icon.

The library review found robot and owl mascots common, while mouse mascots were rare. The mouse direction is recommended for its direct desktop connection and compact silhouette.
